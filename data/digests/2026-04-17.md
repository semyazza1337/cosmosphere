## Wide Jets or Low Rates: Reconciling Short GRB and Gravitational-Wave Neutron Star Merger Rates

**Score:** 7/10  
**arXiv:** [2604.05046](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05046)  
**Authors:** Keerthi Kunnumkai, Antonella Palmese, Brendan O'Connor, Amanda Farah, Ignacio Magana Hernandez
